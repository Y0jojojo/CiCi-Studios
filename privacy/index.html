Meta Pixel and what it does

Our only analytics tool is Meta Pixel (Pixel ID 4484117138583431). We use it to count website visits and understand whether people are reaching our pages. We currently send the standard PageView event; we do not use the Pixel on this site to record purchases, sign-ups, or other custom conversion events.

When enabled, Meta Pixel may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as the page visited, time of the visit, referring page, IP address, browser and device information, and identifiers stored by Meta. Meta may use or combine this information under its own terms and privacy practices. See Meta’s Privacy Policy and Meta Business Tools Terms.

We use the measurement information available through our Meta account to understand visits. What reports are available depends on Meta’s tools and account settings.

Your choice and legal basis

Meta Pixel is blocked until you actively select “Accept optional cookies.” If you reject, the Pixel is not loaded. We rely on your consent for this optional tracking. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings button at the bottom of the page; changing your choice does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal.

Your choice is stored in this browser’s local storage so the site can remember it. This preference record is stored on your device and is not sent to Meta. You can also clear it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Recipients and international transfers

If you accept, information is sent to Meta. Meta operates internationally and may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in countries that may not provide the same level of data protection. The specific Meta entity, processing locations, and transfer safeguards depend on the Meta products and account arrangements in use. Review Meta’s current privacy and business-tools information linked above.

Retention

CiCi Studios does not set a separate retention period for information collected by Meta Pixel. Meta’s retention of identifiers and information it receives is governed by Meta’s settings and policies. We retain the local cookie preference in your browser until you change it or clear site data.

Your rights

Depending on your circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to receive data in a portable format. You may withdraw consent at any time through Cookie settings. Contact CiCi Studios to ask about exercising your rights. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, including the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Meta may separately be responsible for some processing it performs. You can find information about contacting Meta and exercising rights in Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Website hosting and fonts

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. When your browser requests a page or file, GitHub may process technical connection information to deliver the site and protect its service; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The site also loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to retrieve those font files, which may involve processing technical request information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not use Google Fonts for analytics.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice if our website or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
