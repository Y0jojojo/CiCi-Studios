Why we gather

Creativity moves when we make room for it.

CiCi Studios is a home for musicians, thinkers, listeners, and makers. A place to come together, follow a question, share a sound, and leave with something changed.

We believe art can be purposeful without being predictable. Here, dissonance belongs, ideas can take their time, and different voices make the room more alive.
