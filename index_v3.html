What connects us

A space to create, show, and tell.

We believe creativity grows through exchange. A conversation changes a song; a new perspective changes the room. CiCi is a place to gather, learn, express, and make work with consequence.

Rooted in Berlin and open to the world, we make space for curious collaborations across sound, disciplines, and lived experience. Nothing needs to fit the expected shape to belong here.
