Office space details

Workspace and setup

Our bright 21 m² office, with 3.5-meter ceilings, sits inside a 210 m² creative studio in a quiet courtyard on the third floor, with elevator access and plenty of natural light.

The full space is available to rent. If several people are interested, we can set up workstations and arrange separate rentals. High-speed internet is available via Wi-Fi or cable.

A flexible workspace for visual artists, music managers, labels, editors, and other digital creatives.

Shared amenities

  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • Two bathrooms, one with a shower
  • Common areas cleaned once a week
  • On-site parking upon request
  • Basement storage available under a separate contract
  • High-speed Wi-Fi, with cable available
