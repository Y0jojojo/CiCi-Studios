# CiCi Studios brand reference

This document records CiCi Studios' mission, vision, and pillars as a reference
for future site design and development.

## Place

CiCi Studios is based in Berlin. Let the city's international, independent,
experimental creative energy inform the work, while grounding the brand in
CiCi's own community and purpose rather than relying on Berlin clichés.

## Mission

We exist to host a community of intentional creators — musicians, thinkers, and
listeners — who gather to learn, express, and transform. Through workshops,
recordings, and performances, we nurture art that moves with consequence.

## Vision

We envision a space where creativity moves with consequence. Here, art is
purposeful, sound is political, and dissonance is welcomed. We believe that
progressions need both change and permanence (Safety and friction?), and that
true expression emerges from the tension between order and cacophony. Our
studio is a place to gather, to learn, to express: this is a sound lab for the
weird, the intentional, and the socially attuned.

We envision a space that feeds, showcases, and empowers its community. A space
to create, show, and tell. That doesn't limit itself to what's popular or to
what's expected, but dares to expand the view and taste of its community by
hosting and nurturing innovative creatives who push boundaries and want to
create impact.

## Pillars

- **Community** — Make room for people to gather, learn, create, and share.
- **Intention** — Favor purposeful expression and meaningful creative work.
- **Curation** — Introduce work that expands the community's view and taste.
- **Inclusion** — Welcome the weird, the dissonant, and perspectives beyond the expected.

## Design and development direction

Use these as guiding principles when making brand and web decisions:

- Make the experience feel like a creative gathering place and sound lab, not a
  generic corporate studio.
- Balance structure and experimentation; let order and cacophony coexist.
- Prioritize clarity and accessibility while leaving room for expressive,
  unexpected details.
- Present community, creative work, and meaningful impact as central to the
  story.
- For new offerings, such as office rentals, keep the practical information
  clear without losing the character of CiCi Studios.

## Branding directions to explore

These are creative starting points, not mutually exclusive rules. Each should
still feel welcoming and legible to an international Berlin community.

### 1. The Listening Room

**Idea:** A calm, tactile home for intentional listening, learning, and exchange.

- **Visual language:** Warm off-whites, deep ink, muted clay or ochre accents;
  generous negative space; close-up photography of instruments, hands, and
  shared moments.
- **Typography and layout:** Editorial type paired with a clean sans serif.
  Clear hierarchy and unhurried layouts make the work and people the focus.
- **Motion and sound:** Subtle, slow transitions; use sound only when invited,
  never as an automatic effect.
- **Best expresses:** Community and intention; a grounded, welcoming first
  impression for workshops, performances, and rentals.

### 2. Signal / Noise

**Idea:** A sound lab where order and cacophony meet, and purposeful
experimentation is made visible.

- **Visual language:** High-contrast black and cream with one vivid signal
  color; grain, waveform-inspired forms, rhythmic grids, and deliberate
  disruption.
- **Typography and layout:** Strong condensed or mono accents alongside highly
  readable body text. Asymmetry and layered compositions can create friction
  without obscuring essential information.
- **Motion and sound:** Measured pulses and responsive visual details; avoid
  distracting loops or autoplay audio.
- **Best expresses:** Creative experimentation, political sound, and the
  tension between structure and dissonance.

### 3. The Open Circuit

**Idea:** A living network that connects artists, neighbors, disciplines, and
ideas across Berlin and beyond.

- **Visual language:** A flexible system of lines, nodes, annotations, and
  modular color blocks. Use real community portraits and event documentation
  to keep the system human.
- **Typography and layout:** Modular, adaptable page sections with clear
  navigation and multilingual-ready spacing. The identity can shift across
  events while retaining recognizable core elements.
- **Motion and sound:** Connections can appear or rearrange gently as visitors
  explore; every effect should support orientation and accessibility.
- **Best expresses:** Inclusion, curation, and a community that keeps growing
  through collaboration and exchange.
