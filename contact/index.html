The principles behind the practice

What moves us.

01 / Our mission

We exist to host a community of intentional creators — musicians, thinkers, and listeners — who gather to learn, express, and transform. Through workshops, recordings, and performances, we nurture art that moves with consequence.

02 / Our vision

We envision a space where creativity moves with consequence. Here, art is purposeful, sound is political, and dissonance is welcomed. We believe that progressions need both change and permanence (Safety and friction?), and that true expression emerges from the tension between order and cacophony. Our studio is a place to gather, to learn, to express: this is a sound lab for the weird, the intentional, and the socially attuned.

We envision a space that feeds, showcases, and empowers its community. A space to create, show, and tell. That doesn't limit itself to what's popular or to what's expected, but dares to expand the view and taste of its community by hosting and nurturing innovative creatives who push boundaries and want to create impact.

A little about us

Two friends.
One shared frequency.

CiCi began · October 2025

We’re Guillermo and Sebastian. We met in high school, and the connection we built there grew into a shared idea: to make room for artists and the people who bring their work to life.

In October 2025, we began turning that idea into CiCi Studios. We’re building it step by step, with the help, trust, and generosity of wonderful humans who believe in what this can become.

Our dream is to grow an international network of professional artists, connected across Berlin, Colombia, and New York — a community with room to meet, make, and move ideas across borders.
